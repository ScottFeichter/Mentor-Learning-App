# Network Basics

&nbsp;

## Communications

The imparting or exchanging of information.

In general there must be an agreement on the following things to facilitate communication:

- Method
- Language
- Confirmation

&nbsp;

---

&nbsp;

## Telecommunications

The process of exchanging information across a distance by electromagnetic methods.

Three types of signal transmission:

- Electrical: via electronic pulses on copper wire
- Optical: via light pulses on fiber cable (glass or plastic)
- Wireless: via infrared, microwave, or radio waves in air

&nbsp;

---

&nbsp;

## Networking

Involves facilitating the transmission of signals between interconnected entities.
