# Document Shortcuts

## Text Formatting

- **⌘B** — Bold
- **⌘I** — Italic
- **⌘U** — Underline
- **⌘K** — Add web link
- **⌘T** — Show/hide Fonts window
- **⌘{** — Left align
- **⌘}** — Right align
- **⇧⌘|** — Center align

## Deletion

- **⌥Delete** — Delete word to the left
- **⌃H** — Delete character to the left
- **⌃D** — Delete character to the right (or Fn-Delete)
- **⌃K** — Delete to end of line/paragraph

## Cursor Movement

- **⌘↑** — Move to beginning of document
- **⌘↓** — Move to end of document
- **⌘←** — Move to beginning of line
- **⌘→** — Move to end of line
- **⌥←** — Move to beginning of previous word
- **⌥→** — Move to end of next word
- **⌃A** — Move to beginning of line/paragraph
- **⌃E** — Move to end of line/paragraph

## Selection

- **⇧⌘↑** — Select to beginning of document
- **⇧⌘↓** — Select to end of document
- **⇧⌘←** — Select to beginning of line
- **⇧⌘→** — Select to end of line
- **⇧↑/↓/←/→** — Extend selection one character/line
- **⌥⇧←/→** — Extend selection one word
- **⌥⇧↑/↓** — Extend selection one paragraph

## Scrolling

- **Fn↑** — Page Up
- **Fn↓** — Page Down
- **Fn←** — Scroll to beginning (Home)
- **Fn→** — Scroll to end (End)

## Other

- **⌥⌘F** — Go to search field
- **⌥⌘T** — Show/hide toolbar
- **⌥⌘C** — Copy Style
- **⌥⌘V** — Paste Style
- **⌥⇧⌘V** — Paste and Match Style
- **⇧⌘P** — Page setup
- **⇧⌘S** — Save As / Duplicate
- **⇧⌘?** — Open Help menu
