# Switches vs Routers

&nbsp;

## The Key Difference

- **Routing** takes place on the internet (WAN) and uses **IP addresses**
- **Switching** takes place on a LAN and uses **MAC addresses**

&nbsp;

---

&nbsp;

## Ethernet Switches

- Operate at **Layer 2 (Data Link)** of the OSI model
- Use MAC address tables
- Take in a message on a port based on the sending MAC
- Use an address table to send it out a different port based on the destination MAC
