# Setting Up Chrome

&nbsp;

These steps are highly recommended for after download and install of the application.

## Chrome As Default Browser

This helps for example a link in an email will open Chrome rather than a different browser.

## Open Chrome At Login

This makes Chrome open automatically when you turn on or log on to your computer.

### Win

Go to Start > Settings > Apps > Startup and toggle apps to On or Off

### Mac

Go to the Apple menu > System Settings > General > Login Items, then add or remove items under "Open at Login"

### Linux


## Pin To Taskbar or Keep In Dock

Right click on taskbar(Windows) or dock (Mac) and follow the menu selections.

## Sign In

An advantage to signing in is the ability to port settings and bookmarks from elsewhere.

## Import Data

A prompt may appear for you to import bookmarks, saved passwords, and history from an old web browser.
