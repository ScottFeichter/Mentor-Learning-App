# AI

&nbsp;

You likely have an idea of what AI refers to even if you don't understand it.

If not you probably live under a rock.

Not that there is anything wrong with that.

## Do I Need AI To Use Node?

No.

In fact, learning Node without AI will make you much better at leveraging it later.

## Should I Use AI In This Course?

No.

You won't learn and comprehend well if you use AI during this course.

## Should I Use AI In The Wild?

Yes.

AI can be an efficiency tool but it won't make a bad programmer a good programmer.

You must always weight the reality when using AI to determine if the AI is actually adding value.
