# Sleep, Log Out, and Shut Down

- **Power button** — Press to turn on/wake. Hold 1.5s to sleep. Continue holding to force off.
- **⌥⌘ Power** — Put Mac to sleep
- **⌃⇧ Power** — Put displays to sleep
- **⌃ Power** — Dialog: restart, sleep, or shut down
- **⌃⌘ Power** — Force restart (no save prompt)
- **⌃⌘ Media Eject** — Quit all apps, then restart (prompts to save)
- **⌃⌥⌘ Power** — Quit all apps, then shut down (prompts to save)
- **⌃⌘Q** — Immediately lock screen
- **⇧⌘Q** — Log out (with confirmation)
- **⌥⇧⌘Q** — Log out immediately (no confirmation)
