# Wireless Networks

## 802.11 and Wi-Fi

- **IEEE 802** — networking protocols for LAN and MAN (first developed February 1980)
- **802.11** — the amendment concerning Wireless Local Area Networks (WLAN)
- **Wi-Fi** — trademarked term owned by the Wi-Fi Alliance for products conforming to 802.11
- Wi-Fi is NOT a portmanteau of "Wireless Fidelity" — it's just a brand name

## LAN Device Types

- **Hosts** — servers, clients, P2P
- **Peripherals** — shared printers, etc.
- **Network Devices** — intermediary devices (switches, routers)
