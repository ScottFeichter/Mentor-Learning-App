# Design Fundamentals

## Why Design Matters

- Internet users are everyone — totally diverse
- A poor color choice can make a website unusable for 300 million people

## Dieter Rams's 10 Principles for Good Design

Good design...

1. Is innovative
2. Makes a product useful
3. Is aesthetic
4. Makes a product understandable
5. Is unobtrusive
6. Is honest
7. Is long-lasting
8. Is thorough down to the last detail
9. Is environmentally friendly
10. Is minimal

## Industrial Design and the Modern Web

- The influence of [Rams's Braun appliances on Ive's Apple designs](https://www.cultofmac.com/188753/the-braun-products-that-inspired-apples-iconic-designs-gallery/) is clear
- **Skeuomorphic Design** — styling digital components to resemble physical components (e.g., first iOS)
- **Flat Design** — uses flat colors and thin, solid outlines (e.g., iOS 7)

## Modern Web Design Principles

### White Space

- Leaving empty space surrounding text, images, and elements
- Increases legibility and usability
- Especially important on mobile

### Colors

**Aesthetics:**
- Colors should complement, not clash
- Tools: [Adobe Color Wheel](https://color.adobe.com/create/color-wheel), [Google Material Design](https://material.io/design/color/the-color-system.html)

**Accessibility:**
- Colors should be easily distinguishable even for color-blind users
- Colors should be distinct in hue, saturation, and value
- Test with [Color Blindness Simulator](https://www.color-blindness.com/coblis-color-blindness-simulator/)

### Typography

- **Serif fonts** — have small strokes (Times New Roman, Georgia)
- **Sans serif fonts** — do not have small strokes (Helvetica, Roboto)

### Conventions

- Be aware of common conventions (e.g., hamburger menu icon)
