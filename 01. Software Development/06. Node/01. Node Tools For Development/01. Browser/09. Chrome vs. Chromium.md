# Chrome vs. Chromium

&nbsp;

**Chrome** and **Chromium** are closely related but not the same thing.

## Chromium

<div class="indent">

### What It Is

**Chromium** is the open source project that **Chrome** is built on.

Anyone can view, download, and build the source code.

### Who Maintains It

Google leads development.

Contributions come from many companies and individuals.

### What It Lacks

**Chromium** does not include proprietary Google features.

This means no automatic updates, certain codecs, crash reporting, or Google branding.

</div>

## Chrome

<div class="indent">

### What It Is

**Chrome** is Google's finished, proprietary browser built on top of **Chromium**.

It adds Google-specific features on top of the open source base.

### What It Adds

- Automatic updates
- Google account sync
- Additional media codecs
- Widevine DRM for streaming services
- Google branding and crash reporting

</div>

## The Relationship

<div class="indent">

### Analogy

**Chromium** is the engine and frame.

**Chrome** is the finished car with Google's features bolted on.

### Why It Matters

Many browsers — Edge, Opera, Brave — are also built on **Chromium**.

This is why they share the same rendering engine (Blink) and JS engine (V8) as **Chrome**.

</div>
