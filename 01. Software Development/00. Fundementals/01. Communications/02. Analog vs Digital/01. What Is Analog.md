# What Is Analog?

&nbsp;

An analog signal is a continuous signal that varies smoothly over time.

It can take any value within a range — there are no discrete steps or jumps.

## Examples

- Sound waves traveling through air
- The voltage output of a microphone
- A traditional clock with hands
- A vinyl record

## How It Works

Analog signals represent information as a continuously varying physical quantity — voltage, pressure, light intensity.

The shape of the wave itself carries the information.

## Advantages and Limitations

- <span class="olive-bold">Advantage</span> — naturally represents real world phenomena which are inherently continuous
- <span class="olive-bold">Limitation</span> — susceptible to noise and interference, which degrades the signal over distance
