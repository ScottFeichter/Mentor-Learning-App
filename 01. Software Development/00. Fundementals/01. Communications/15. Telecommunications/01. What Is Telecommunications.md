# What Is Telecommunications?

&nbsp;

Telecommunications is the transmission of information over distances using electronic and electromagnetic systems.

The word comes from the Greek *tele* meaning far and the Latin *communicare* meaning to share.

## What It Covers

Telecommunications encompasses any technology that transmits signals over distance:

- Telephone networks
- Radio and television broadcasting
- Mobile and cellular networks
- Satellite communication
- Internet infrastructure
- Fiber optic networks

## Telecommunications vs Networking

Telecommunications provides the physical infrastructure and transmission systems.

Networking is the logical layer that runs on top of that infrastructure — addressing, routing, and delivering data between specific devices.

## Why It Matters

Telecommunications is the foundation of all modern communication.

Every phone call, internet connection, and broadcast signal depends on telecommunications infrastructure.
