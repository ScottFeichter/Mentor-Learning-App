# What Is Optical Communication?

&nbsp;

Optical communication is the transmission of information as pulses of light rather than as electrical signals.

Light travels faster and with less energy loss than electrical signals in copper wire, making optical communication ideal for high speed, long distance data transmission.

## How It Works

A transmitter converts an electrical signal into pulses of light using a laser or LED.

Those pulses travel through a fiber optic cable — a thin strand of glass or plastic that guides light along its length using total internal reflection.

A receiver at the other end converts the light pulses back into an electrical signal.

## Why Light?

- Light travels at extremely high speeds through fiber
- Very low signal loss compared to copper — signals can travel hundreds of kilometers without amplification
- Immune to electromagnetic interference
- Extremely high bandwidth capacity

## Applications

- Undersea cables connecting continents
- Internet backbone infrastructure
- Data center interconnects
- Fiber to the home (FTTH) broadband
- Medical imaging equipment
