# Radio

&nbsp;

Radio was the first technology to transmit information wirelessly over long distances using electromagnetic waves.

## Development

- <span class="olive-bold">1864</span> — James Clerk Maxwell theorized the existence of electromagnetic waves
- <span class="olive-bold">1887</span> — Heinrich Hertz demonstrated radio waves experimentally
- <span class="olive-bold">1895</span> — Guglielmo Marconi transmitted the first radio signals over distance
- <span class="olive-bold">1901</span> — Marconi transmitted the first transatlantic radio signal

## How It Worked

A transmitter converted audio into electromagnetic waves and broadcast them via an antenna.

Any receiver tuned to the same frequency could pick up the signal.

Unlike the telegraph and telephone, radio required no physical connection between sender and receiver.

## Broadcasting

Radio introduced the concept of broadcasting — one transmitter reaching an unlimited number of receivers simultaneously.

Commercial radio broadcasting began in the early 1920s and rapidly became a mass medium for news, entertainment, and music.

## Impact

- First mass medium for real time news and entertainment
- Transformed military communication in World War I and II
- Established the electromagnetic spectrum as a shared resource requiring regulation
- The underlying technology — wireless transmission via radio waves — is the foundation of Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular networks, and GPS
