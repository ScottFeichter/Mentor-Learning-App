# Tradeoffs

&nbsp;

Serial and parallel communication each have strengths and weaknesses that make them suited to different contexts.

## Serial Advantages Over Parallel

- Fewer wires means simpler, cheaper cables and connectors
- Less susceptible to crosstalk and timing skew at high speeds
- Scales better over long distances
- Modern serial interfaces like USB and SATA achieve very high speeds despite sending one bit at a time

## Parallel Advantages Over Serial

- Theoretically higher throughput at the same clock speed since multiple bits travel simultaneously
- Simpler logic at low speeds and short distances

## Why Serial Won

At low speeds parallel was faster in practice.

But as clock speeds increased, keeping multiple wires in sync became increasingly difficult.

Tiny differences in wire length and electrical properties cause bits to arrive at slightly different times — called skew — which causes errors.

Serial communication avoids this entirely by using a single channel, and modern encoding techniques allow serial interfaces to achieve speeds that far exceed older parallel standards.

This is why USB replaced parallel printer ports, SATA replaced PATA, and PCIe replaced PCI.
