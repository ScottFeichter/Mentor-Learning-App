# What Is Serial Communication?

&nbsp;

Serial communication is a method of transmitting data one bit at a time over a single channel.

Bits are sent sequentially — one after another — along a single wire or signal path.

## How It Works

The sender converts data into a stream of individual bits and transmits them one by one.

The receiver reassembles the bits back into the original data.

## Examples

- <span class="olive-bold">USB</span> — Universal Serial Bus
- <span class="olive-bold">SATA</span> — used to connect hard drives
- <span class="olive-bold">Ethernet</span> — network communication over a cable
- <span class="olive-bold">UART</span> — used in embedded systems and microcontrollers

## Advantages

- Requires fewer wires than parallel communication
- Less susceptible to timing issues at high speeds
- Simpler and cheaper to implement over long distances
