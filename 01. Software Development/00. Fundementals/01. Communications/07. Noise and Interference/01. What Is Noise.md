# What Is Noise?

&nbsp;

In communications, noise is any unwanted signal that gets added to a transmission and distorts or obscures the original information.

## Types of Noise

- <span class="olive-bold">Thermal Noise</span> — caused by the random motion of electrons in a conductor, present in all electronic systems
- <span class="olive-bold">Crosstalk</span> — interference from a signal in an adjacent wire or channel bleeding into another
- <span class="olive-bold">Impulse Noise</span> — sudden spikes caused by external events like lightning or power surges
- <span class="olive-bold">Intermodulation Noise</span> — caused by signals of different frequencies mixing and producing unwanted frequencies

## Effect on Communication

Noise degrades signal quality.

In analog systems it manifests as static, hiss, or distortion.

In digital systems it can cause bit errors — a 0 being read as a 1 or vice versa.

## Managing Noise

- Shielding cables reduces electromagnetic interference
- Error detection and correction codes allow digital systems to detect and fix bit errors caused by noise
- Increasing signal strength improves the signal to noise ratio
