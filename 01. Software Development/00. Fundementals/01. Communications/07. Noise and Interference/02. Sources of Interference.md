# Sources of Interference

&nbsp;

Interference is any external signal or phenomenon that disrupts a communication channel.

## Electromagnetic Interference (EMI)

Caused by electromagnetic fields from nearby devices — motors, power lines, fluorescent lights, and other electronics.

Can induce unwanted currents in cables and circuits.

## Radio Frequency Interference (RFI)

A subset of EMI specifically involving radio frequency signals.

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, microwaves, and cellular signals can all interfere with each other when operating on overlapping frequencies.

## Physical Interference

- <span class="olive-bold">Attenuation</span> — signal weakening over distance as energy is absorbed by the medium
- <span class="olive-bold">Reflection</span> — signals bouncing off surfaces and arriving at the receiver at different times
- <span class="olive-bold">Absorption</span> — walls, water, and other materials absorbing wireless signals

## Environmental Interference

Weather conditions like rain, fog, and atmospheric disturbances can affect wireless and satellite communications.

## Mitigation

- Shielded cables reduce EMI
- Frequency planning reduces RFI between wireless systems
- Signal repeaters and amplifiers combat attenuation over long distances
