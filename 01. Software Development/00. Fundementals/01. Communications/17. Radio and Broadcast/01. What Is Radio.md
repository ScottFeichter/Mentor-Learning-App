# What Is Radio?

&nbsp;

Radio is the transmission of information through electromagnetic waves in the radio frequency range of the spectrum.

Radio waves travel at the speed of light and can propagate through the atmosphere without a physical medium.

## How It Works

A transmitter converts an electrical signal into radio waves and broadcasts them via an antenna.

A receiver picks up those waves with its own antenna and converts them back into an electrical signal.

## The Radio Frequency Spectrum

Radio frequencies range from about 3 Hz to 300 GHz.

Different frequency bands are used for different purposes — AM radio, FM radio, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, and radar all occupy different parts of the spectrum.

## Uses of Radio

- AM and FM broadcasting
- Television broadcasting
- Mobile and cellular networks
- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
- GPS
- Radar
- Satellite communication
- Emergency services

## Why It Matters

Radio is the foundation of all wireless communication.

Every wireless technology — from a phone call to a Wi-Fi connection — uses radio waves to transmit information through the air.
