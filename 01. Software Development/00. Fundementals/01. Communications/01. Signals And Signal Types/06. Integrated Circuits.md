# Integrated Circuits

&nbsp;

An integrated circuit (IC) is a set of electronic components — transistors, resistors, capacitors — miniaturized and fabricated together on a single piece of semiconductor material, typically silicon.

## How It Works

Before integrated circuits, electronic components were individual parts wired together by hand.

An IC etches all of those components and their connections directly onto a tiny chip of silicon using a photographic process.

This allows billions of components to fit in a space smaller than a fingernail.

## Types of Integrated Circuits

- <span class="olive-bold">Microprocessor</span> — a CPU on a single chip, executes program instructions
- <span class="olive-bold">Memory chips</span> — store data <span class="muted">(RAM, ROM, flash)</span>
- <span class="olive-bold">Logic chips</span> — perform specific logical or arithmetic operations
- <span class="olive-bold">Microcontrollers</span> — a processor, memory, and I/O on a single chip <span class="muted">(used in embedded systems)</span>

## Why It Matters

The integrated circuit is what made computers small, fast, and affordable.

Every device with computing capability — phones, laptops, cars, appliances — contains integrated circuits.

The CPU in your computer is an integrated circuit containing billions of transistors working together to execute code.
