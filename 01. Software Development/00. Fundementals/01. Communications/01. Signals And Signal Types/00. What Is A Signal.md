# What Is A Signal?

&nbsp;

A signal is a physical carrier of information.

It is a change in some measurable quantity — voltage, light, sound, or radio waves — that represents data.

## How It Works

Information cannot travel on its own.

It must be encoded into a physical medium that can move through space or along a wire.

A signal is that encoding — the physical representation of information in transit.

## Types of Signals

- <span class="olive-bold">Analog</span> — a continuous wave that varies smoothly over time <span class="muted">(sound waves, radio)</span>
- <span class="olive-bold">Digital</span> — a signal that switches between discrete states <span class="muted">(on/off, 1/0)</span>

## Why It Matters

All computing and communication begins with signals.

Every piece of data stored or transmitted by a computer is ultimately a pattern of electrical signals.
