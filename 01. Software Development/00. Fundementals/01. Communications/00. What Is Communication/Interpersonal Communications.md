# Interpersonal Communications

&nbsp;

The branch of communications concerned with how individuals exchange information, meaning, and emotion directly with one another.

## Subfields

- <span class="olive-bold">Linguistics</span> — the structure and rules of human language
- <span class="olive-bold">Rhetoric</span> — the art of effective and persuasive speaking and writing
- <span class="olive-bold">Nonverbal Communication</span> — body language, tone, gesture, and expression
- <span class="olive-bold">Psychology of Communication</span> — how perception, emotion, and cognition shape exchanges
- <span class="olive-bold">Cross-Cultural Communication</span> — how meaning is transmitted across different cultural contexts
- <span class="olive-bold">Conflict Resolution</span> — communication strategies for navigating disagreement
