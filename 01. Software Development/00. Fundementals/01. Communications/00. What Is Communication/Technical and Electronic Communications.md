# Technical and Electronic Communications

&nbsp;

The branch of communications concerned with how information is encoded, transmitted, and processed by machines and electronic systems.

This is the branch that gave rise to Computer Science and the modern internet.

## Subfields

- <span class="olive-bold">Signals and Electricity</span> — how information is physically encoded and transmitted
- <span class="olive-bold">Computer Science</span> — computation, algorithms, software, and programming languages
- <span class="olive-bold">Information Technology</span> — the practical deployment and management of computing systems
- <span class="olive-bold">Networks</span> — how devices are connected and how data moves between them
- <span class="olive-bold">Protocols</span> — the agreed upon rules governing how systems communicate
- <span class="olive-bold">Cybersecurity</span> — protecting electronic systems and the information they carry
- <span class="olive-bold">Artificial Intelligence</span> — systems that process and respond to information autonomously
