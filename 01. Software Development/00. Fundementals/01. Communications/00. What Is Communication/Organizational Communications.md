# Organizational Communications

&nbsp;

The branch of communications concerned with how information flows within and between organizations.

## Subfields

- <span class="olive-bold">Internal Communications</span> — how information moves between people and teams inside an organization
- <span class="olive-bold">Leadership Communication</span> — how direction, vision, and decisions are conveyed from leadership
- <span class="olive-bold">Change Management</span> — communicating transitions and shifts in strategy or structure
- <span class="olive-bold">Crisis Communications</span> — managing information flow during emergencies or reputational threats
- <span class="olive-bold">Knowledge Management</span> — how organizations capture, store, and share institutional knowledge
- <span class="olive-bold">Organizational Culture</span> — the role communication plays in shaping shared values and norms
