# What Is Bandwidth?

&nbsp;

Bandwidth is the maximum amount of data that can be transmitted over a communication channel in a given amount of time.

It is typically measured in bits per second — kilobits, megabits, or gigabits per second (Kbps, Mbps, Gbps).

## Analogy

Think of bandwidth like the width of a pipe.

A wider pipe can carry more water at once — a higher bandwidth connection can carry more data at once.

## Bandwidth vs Speed

Bandwidth is often confused with speed but they are not the same.

Bandwidth is the capacity of the channel — how much data it can carry.

Speed refers to how fast data actually travels, which is affected by latency and other factors.

## Why It Matters

Higher bandwidth means more data can be transferred simultaneously.

It is a key factor in the performance of networks, internet connections, and communication systems.
