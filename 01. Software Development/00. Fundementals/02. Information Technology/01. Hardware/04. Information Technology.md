# Information Technology

&nbsp;

The use of computers and software to store, manage, and transmit information within organizations and systems.

Where Computer Science asks *how do we build this*, IT asks *how do we use and maintain this*.

## Where It Sits

Information Technology is the most applied layer.

It takes what Computer Science builds and puts it to practical use in real-world environments.

## What It Covers

- <span class="olive-bold">Systems Administration</span> — managing servers, networks, and hardware
- <span class="olive-bold">Databases</span> — storing and retrieving organized data
- <span class="olive-bold">Security</span> — protecting systems and data from unauthorized access
- <span class="olive-bold">Support</span> — maintaining and troubleshooting technology in use
- <span class="olive-bold">Software Deployment</span> — getting software running in real environments
