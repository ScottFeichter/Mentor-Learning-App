# Wireless Networks

&nbsp;

## 802.11 and Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi refers to technology for transmitting information using radio waves, based on standards established by the **IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)**.

IEEE 802.11 came to be commonly referred to as Wi-Fi. The **Wi-Fi Alliance** provides testing and certification for products conforming to IEEE 802.11.

## Wireless Network Types

- **WPAN** — Wireless Personal Area Network (e.g. Bluetooth, short range)
- **WLAN** — Wireless Local Area Network — IEEE 802.11 (radio waves, short-medium range)
- **WMAN** — Wireless Metropolitan Area Network
- **WWAN** — Wireless Wide Area Network — large range internetworks
