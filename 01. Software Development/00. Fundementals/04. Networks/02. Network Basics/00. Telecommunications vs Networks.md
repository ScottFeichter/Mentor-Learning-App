# Telecommunications vs Networks

&nbsp;

These two fields overlap heavily but each has a distinct focus.

## Telecommunications

The broader, older field.

Concerned with transmitting signals over distance using any medium — telephone lines, radio waves, satellite, and fiber.

Includes the physical infrastructure and the engineering of transmission systems.

## Networks

More specific in scope.

Concerned with how devices are interconnected and how data is addressed, routed, and delivered between them.

It is the logical layer that sits on top of the physical transmission infrastructure that telecommunications provides.

## The Key Distinction

Telecommunications gets the signal from point A to point B.

Networks determines which point B to send it to and how to get it there reliably.

## How They Relate Today

In practice the two fields have converged significantly.

Modern telecommunications is largely digital and network-based, which is why the two are easy to conflate.

Telecommunications provides the physical medium.

Networks provide the logic that runs on top of it.
