# Video Cards, Capture Cards, and NICs

## Video Cards (Graphics Adapters)

Generates the signal to drive a monitor or projector.

- **Onboard graphics** — included with motherboard chipset or CPU (low-end)
- **Add-on card** — installed via PCIe slot (for gaming, CAD, digital artwork)
- Chipsets by: ATI/AMD, NVIDIA, Intel

### Key Features

- **GPU** — microprocessor optimized for rendering 2D/3D images and effects
- **Graphics memory** — dedicated GDDR RAM (up to 12 GB high-end, 4–6 GB mid-range)
  - Low-end cards use shared system RAM
- **Video ports** — HDMI, DisplayPort, Thunderbolt
- Most modern cards use **PCIe x16** interface
- Dual cards using two or more slots are available

## Capture Cards

Records video input and saves it as movie/streaming media files.

- Designed to record from: PC games, console HDMI, live camera HDMI, broadcast TV
- Internal: PCIe
- External: USB or Thunderbolt

## Audio Interfaces

- Built-in audio susceptible to noise from internal components
- Professional audio interfaces are typically external (USB or Thunderbolt)

## Network Interface Cards (NICs)

- Most computers have Ethernet built into the motherboard chipset
- Add-on NIC needed for: different network type, different cabling, multiple ports
- Multiple ports can be **bonded** into a single higher bandwidth link
- **Wi-Fi adapters** — connect to wireless networks (various 802.11 standards)
- **Cellular adapters** — connect to cellular data networks
