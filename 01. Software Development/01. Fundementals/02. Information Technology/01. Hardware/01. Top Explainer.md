# Computer Science, IT, and Communications

&nbsp;

These three fields are closely related but each has a distinct focus.

## Computer Science

The study of computation — how problems are solved using algorithms, data structures, and software.

It is concerned with the theory and practice of writing programs that instruct computers to perform tasks.

## Information Technology

The use of computers and software to store, manage, and transmit information within organizations and systems.

Where Computer Science asks *how do we build this*, IT asks *how do we use and maintain this*.

## Communications

The study and practice of transmitting information between systems and people.

In a technical context this includes signals, networks, protocols, and the infrastructure that connects devices across the world.

## How They Relate

<div class="indent">

- <span class="olive-bold">Computer Science</span> builds the tools and systems
- <span class="olive-bold">Information Technology</span> deploys and manages those systems
- <span class="olive-bold">Communications</span> connects those systems together

</div>

Each layer depends on the one beneath it.

Communications provides the infrastructure, Computer Science provides the logic, and IT puts it all to work.
