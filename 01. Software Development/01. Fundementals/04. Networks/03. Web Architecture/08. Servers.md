# Servers

&nbsp;

A **server** is defined by what it does.

## What Does It Do?

A **server** pushes and pulls data across a network.

Its purpose is to send data to a client by request.

## Hardware Or Software?

The word **server** can refer to the hardware that performs this task.

The word **server** can also refer to software that programs the hardware to serve.

### Context Dependant

The meaning of the word **server** will be dependant on the context it is used.

#### Hardware Server

- Physically a hardware **server** is just a computer.

- It differs from a personal computer in that its specs have been optimized for serving.

- You can shop and buy a hardware server as you would a personal computer.
