# Coaxial Cable

&nbsp;

Coaxial cable is a type of guided transmission medium consisting of a central copper conductor surrounded by insulation, a metallic shield, and an outer jacket.

The shield reduces electromagnetic interference, making it more resistant to noise than twisted pair.

## Structure

- <span class="olive-bold">Inner conductor</span> — carries the signal
- <span class="olive-bold">Dielectric insulator</span> — separates the conductor from the shield
- <span class="olive-bold">Metallic shield</span> — blocks external interference
- <span class="olive-bold">Outer jacket</span> — physical protection

## Advantages

- Better shielding than twisted pair
- Supports higher bandwidth over longer distances than twisted pair
- Durable and resistant to interference

## Limitations

- Bulkier and less flexible than twisted pair
- More expensive to install
- Largely replaced by fiber for high speed long distance links

## Common Uses

- Cable television (CATV)
- Cable internet (DOCSIS)
- Antenna connections for TV and radio
- Legacy Ethernet installations
