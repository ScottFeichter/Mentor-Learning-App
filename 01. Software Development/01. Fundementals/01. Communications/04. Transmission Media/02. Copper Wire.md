# Copper Wire

&nbsp;

Copper wire is the oldest and most widely deployed guided transmission medium.

It transmits data as electrical signals through a conductor made of copper.

## Types

- <span class="olive-bold">Twisted Pair</span> — two insulated copper wires twisted together to reduce interference, used in Ethernet and telephone lines
- <span class="olive-bold">Unshielded Twisted Pair (UTP)</span> — most common, used in home and office networks
- <span class="olive-bold">Shielded Twisted Pair (STP)</span> — has additional shielding for environments with high interference

## Advantages

- Inexpensive and widely available
- Easy to install and terminate
- Sufficient for most short to medium distance networking needs

## Limitations

- Susceptible to electromagnetic interference
- Signal degrades over distance — requires repeaters for long runs
- Lower bandwidth ceiling than fiber optics
- Older infrastructure may limit speeds

## Common Uses

- Ethernet (Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6a)
- Telephone lines (DSL)
- Short range data connections within buildings
