# What Is Transmission Media?

&nbsp;

Transmission media is the physical or wireless medium through which data signals travel from one device to another.

It is the channel in the sender-receiver model — the path the signal takes between source and destination.

## Two Categories

- <span class="olive-bold">Guided media</span> — signals travel along a physical path such as a wire or cable
- <span class="olive-bold">Unguided media</span> — signals travel through the air or space without a physical conductor

## Why It Matters

The choice of transmission medium affects bandwidth, latency, distance, cost, and susceptibility to interference.

Different media are suited to different environments and use cases.
