# Use Cases

&nbsp;

Satellite communication serves a wide range of applications where ground-based infrastructure is impractical or unavailable.

## Broadcasting

Television and radio signals are distributed via satellite to large geographic areas simultaneously.

A single uplink can reach millions of receivers without the need for ground-based relay infrastructure.

## GPS and Navigation

The Global Positioning System (GPS) uses a constellation of MEO satellites to provide precise location data anywhere on Earth.

Other systems include GLONASS (Russia), Galileo (Europe), and BeiDou (China).

## Internet Access

Satellite internet provides broadband access to remote and rural areas where fiber and cable infrastructure does not reach.

LEO constellations like Starlink are making this increasingly viable with lower latency than traditional GEO satellite internet.

## Weather and Earth Observation

Weather satellites monitor atmospheric conditions, cloud cover, and storm systems in real time.

Earth observation satellites are used for agriculture, disaster response, environmental monitoring, and military reconnaissance.

## Maritime and Aviation

Ships and aircraft use satellite communication for navigation, weather updates, passenger internet, and safety communications in areas beyond the reach of ground-based networks.
