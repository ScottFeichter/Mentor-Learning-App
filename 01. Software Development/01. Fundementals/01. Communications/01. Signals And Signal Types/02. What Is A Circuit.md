# What Is A Circuit?

&nbsp;

A circuit is a closed path through which electricity can flow.

Without a complete path, current cannot move and the circuit does not function.

## How It Works

A circuit requires a power source, a conductor, and a load.

The power source pushes electrons through the conductor.

The load — a component like a light bulb or transistor — uses the energy as the current passes through it.

When the path is broken, current stops flowing.

## Types of Circuits

- <span class="olive-bold">Series</span> — components are connected in a single path <span class="muted">(one break stops everything)</span>
- <span class="olive-bold">Parallel</span> — components are connected across multiple paths <span class="muted">(one break doesn't stop the rest)</span>

## Why It Matters

Every electronic device is built from circuits.

A computer is an enormously complex arrangement of circuits working together to process and transmit electrical signals.
