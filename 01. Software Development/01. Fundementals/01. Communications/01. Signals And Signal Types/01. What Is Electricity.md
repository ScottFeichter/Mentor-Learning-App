# What Is Electricity?

&nbsp;

Electricity is the flow of electric charge through a conductor.

It is the physical phenomenon that powers all electronic devices and carries digital signals.

## How It Works

All matter is made of atoms.

Atoms contain electrons, which carry a negative electric charge.

In certain materials — called conductors — electrons can move freely from atom to atom.

When a force is applied, those electrons flow in a direction, creating an electric current.

## Key Concepts

- <span class="olive-bold">Voltage</span> — the force that pushes electrons through a conductor <span class="muted">(measured in volts)</span>
- <span class="olive-bold">Current</span> — the rate at which electrons flow <span class="muted">(measured in amps)</span>
- <span class="olive-bold">Resistance</span> — how much a material opposes the flow of electrons <span class="muted">(measured in ohms)</span>

## Why It Matters

Electricity is the medium through which digital signals travel.

The on/off states of a digital signal are represented by high and low voltage levels in an electrical circuit.
