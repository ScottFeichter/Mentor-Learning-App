# Mass Communications

&nbsp;

The branch of communications concerned with transmitting information to large audiences through media channels.

## Subfields

- <span class="olive-bold">Journalism</span> — reporting and distributing news and information
- <span class="olive-bold">Broadcasting</span> — transmitting audio and video content via radio and television
- <span class="olive-bold">Publishing</span> — producing and distributing written content at scale
- <span class="olive-bold">Advertising</span> — crafting and distributing persuasive messages to audiences
- <span class="olive-bold">Public Relations</span> — managing the public image of organizations and individuals
- <span class="olive-bold">Digital Media</span> — online platforms, social media, and internet-based content distribution
