# Telecommunications

&nbsp;

The branch of communications concerned with transmitting information over distances using electronic and electromagnetic systems.

## Subfields

- <span class="olive-bold">Telephony</span> — transmitting voice over wired and wireless systems
- <span class="olive-bold">Radio</span> — transmitting information via electromagnetic waves through the air
- <span class="olive-bold">Satellite Communications</span> — relaying signals via orbiting satellites
- <span class="olive-bold">Fiber Optics</span> — transmitting data as light through glass or plastic cables
- <span class="olive-bold">Wireless Networks</span> — Wi-Fi, cellular, and other short and long range wireless systems
- <span class="olive-bold">Broadcasting Infrastructure</span> — the physical systems that distribute signals at scale
