# Communications

&nbsp;

The study and practice of transmitting information between systems and people.

In a technical context this includes signals, networks, protocols, and the infrastructure that connects devices across the world.

## Why It Is The Foundation

Every computer, server, and device is only useful if it can send and receive information.

Communications defines the rules and infrastructure that make that possible.

## What It Covers

- <span class="olive-bold">Signals</span> — how information is encoded and transmitted physically
- <span class="olive-bold">Networks</span> — how devices are connected together
- <span class="olive-bold">Protocols</span> — the agreed upon rules for how data is sent and received
- <span class="olive-bold">Infrastructure</span> — the hardware and systems that carry information across the world
