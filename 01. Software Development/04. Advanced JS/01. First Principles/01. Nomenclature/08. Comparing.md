# Comparing

&nbsp;

Let's compare the ideas we have examined.

---

## Buzz Words / Terms Of Art / Jargon / Semantic Drift

### Term Of Art
**→** precise technical definition, used correctly

### Jargon
**→** insider shorthand, understood within a group

### Buzz Word
**→** vague, overused, signals more than it communicates

### Semantic Drift
**→** a word shifts away from its precise meaning over time

---

## Language Evolves

Words and phrases evolve naturally over time.

### One Pattern Of Many

A **Term Of Art** can become **Jargon** through casual use.

**Jargon** can become a **Buzz Word** when it gets picked up by marketing.

### What Does It Mean?

What began as a **Term Of Art** is no longer functioning as such.

It is stretched beyond its original meaning and **Semantic Drift** occurs.
