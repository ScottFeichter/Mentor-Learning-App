# Jargon

&nbsp;

**Jargon** differs from **Terms Of Art** and can be a gateway to **Semantic Drift**.

---

## What Is Jargon?

Have you ever had to request someone to use "layman's terms"?

### Insider Shorthand

**Jargon** is informal insider language used by members of a group.

It is shorthand words or phrases that carrying meaning.

### People In The Know

But the understanding is only among people in the same field or community.

Outside that group, **jargon** is often confusing or meaningless.

---

## Everyday Example

In the absence of understanding words become **jargon**.

### Medical Jargon

Doctors say a patient is **NPO** before surgery.

**NPO** comes from the Latin *nil per os* — nothing by mouth.


### Audience Dependant

To a doctor or nurse it is instant, precise communication.

To a patient it is meaningless without explanation.

---

## Jargon In Programming

Developers use **jargon** constantly.

### Examples

- "spin up a server"
- "it's blocking"
- "just refactor it"
- "that's a code smell"
- "ship it"
- "it's in prod"

### Who Is In The Know

These phrases are understood immediately by developers.

They are opaque to people outside the field.

---

## Terms Of Art vs Jargon

All **terms of art** can become **jargon**  but not all **jargon** is a **term of art**.

### Familiarity

**Jargon** is about *familiarity*
- insiders understand it
- outsiders don't

### Precision

**Terms of Art** are about *precision*
- they carry an exact technical definition within a field
- it is intended to illuminate understanding in its arena

---

## When Jargon Becomes a Problem

**Jargon** is useful for speed and efficiency among insiders.

### Problematic Jargon

It becomes a problem when:

- it is used with someone who doesn't know it yet
- it replaces a **term of art** and loses precision
- it drifts in meaning across different groups

### Evolution And Drift

This is where **jargon** and **semantic drift** overlap.

A term starts precise, becomes **jargon**, then drifts until the original meaning is lost.
