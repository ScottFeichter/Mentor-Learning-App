# JS Syntactic Parts

&nbsp;

**Syntax** in a programming language refers to what you write.

---

## Incidentals

What you write that the parser ignores.

### Comments

What you write to give notes to other developers.

### Whitespace

What you write to give space and readibility.

---

## Statements

What you write to form a complete instruction for the engine to execute.

### Lexicals

What you write from the fixed vocabulary of the language.

<div class="indent-quarter">

#### Punctuators

What you write to structure and separate code.

#### Keywords

What you write to control the engine.

</div>

### Expressions

What you write that produces a value.

<div class="indent-quarter">

#### Operators

What you write to perform operations.

#### Operands

What you write that operators act on.

<div class="indent-quarter">

##### Identifiers

<div class="indent">

What you write to name things.

</div>

##### Values

<div class="indent">

What you write to store data of named items.

</div>

</div>

</div>
