# Expressions

&nbsp;

**Expressions** are what you write to produce a value.

---

## What Makes Something An Expression

**Expressions** are syntax that can be parsed by JS but are not pre-defined or reserved.

### Expressions Are Statements

All **Expressions** are Statements.

### Not All Statements Are Expressions

**Expressions** are siblings of Lexicals.

---

## Expressions Can Be Nested

An **Expressions** can exist independently or as a part of other entities.

### Connect Or Combine

You can connect or combine **Expressions** and Lexicals to build larger Statements.

### Example

The result of one **Expression** can be the operand of another.

```js
(a + b) * (c + d)       // two expressions nested inside a larger one
```


---

## Kinds Of Expressions

There are two broad categories of **Expressions**.

### Operators

Symbols not belonging to JS but read to perform an operation.

### Operands

Anything an operator acts on.
