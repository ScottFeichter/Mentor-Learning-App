# this

&nbsp;

You may have heard of the <span class="js-yellow">JavaScript</span> keyword **```this```**.

We don't need to examine the **```this```** keyword here.

## this vs globalThis

Just note that the **```this```** keyword is a different entity than the <span class="purple-bold">globalThis</span> object.

These two parts of <span class="js-yellow">JavaScript</span> are not related to each other as concepts for our purposes now.
