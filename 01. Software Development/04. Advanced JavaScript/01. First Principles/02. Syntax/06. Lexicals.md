# Lexicals

&nbsp;

**Lexicals** are what you write from the fixed vocabulary of the language.

---

## What Makes Something A Lexical

Lexicals are the pre-defined symbols and words that belong to JS itself — not invented by the developer.

They do not produce a value. They structure and direct.

There are only two kinds:

- **Punctuators** — fixed symbols that shape the form of code
- **Keywords** — reserved words the engine recognizes and treats specially

---

## Not Expressions

Lexicals are siblings of **Expressions** under **Statements** — not a subset of them.

The distinction is simple: expressions produce a value, lexicals do not.

```js
if (score > 10) { console.log('high'); }
```

- `if`, `{`, `}`, `(`, `)` — lexicals, they shape the statement but produce nothing

---

## Fixed By The Language

You cannot redefine a lexical. You cannot use `if` as a variable name. You cannot invent a new punctuator.

They are the fixed vocabulary — the part of JS that belongs to the language, not to you.
