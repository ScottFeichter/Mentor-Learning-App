# Recent Phenomena

As of writing the current **buzz words** are anything to do with AI.

---

## Buzz Words In Programming

The tech industry produces **buzz words** constantly.

- "cloud-native"
- "AI-powered"
- "blockchain"
- "disruption"
- "scalable"
- "full-stack"
- "agile"
- "10x engineer"

Some of these started as precise terms.

Most have been stretched so far they no longer carry reliable meaning.

---

## Buzz Words vs Terms Of Art vs Jargon

```
Term of art  → precise technical definition, used correctly
Jargon       → insider shorthand, understood within a group
Buzz word    → vague, overused, signals more than it communicates
```

A term of art can become jargon through casual use.

Jargon can become a buzz word when it gets picked up by marketing and stretched beyond its original meaning.

---

## Why This Matters

Buzz words are noise.

When a word means everything it means nothing.

Learning to recognize buzz words — and replace them with precise language — is a sign of technical maturity.

When you hear a buzz word, ask: **what does this actually mean in concrete terms?**

If no one can answer that, it is a buzz word.
