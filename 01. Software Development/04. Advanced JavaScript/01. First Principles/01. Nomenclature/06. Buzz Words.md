# Buzz Words

&nbsp;

A **buzz word** is a term that:
- quickly became relevant
- swiftly gained popularity
- is used to make an impression

---

## Why They Occur

**Buzz words** spread through marketing, job postings, and conference talks.

### Dilution And Drift

Eventually **buzz words** lose — or never had — a precise meaning.

They signal familiarity with a field without communicating anything specific.

---

## Everyday Example

**Synergy** is a buzz word.

### Precision vs Usage

It technically means two things producing a combined effect greater than the sum of their parts.

In practice it is used in business to mean almost anything — collaboration, alignment, etc.

### Low Value

Because of drift it sounds meaningful but communicates very little.

Additionally there are other words such as **Geshtalt** that may be more precise.
