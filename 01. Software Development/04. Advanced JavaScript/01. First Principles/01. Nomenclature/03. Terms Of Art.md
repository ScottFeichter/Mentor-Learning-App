# Terms Of Art

&nbsp;

A **term of art** is a word or phrase used in a certain manner.

It has a precise, agreed-upon meaning within a specific field.

---

## Everyday Example

Outside a field the word may mean something different or nothing at all.

### Consideration

The word **consideration** is a good example.

Its meaning varies considerablly between its legal and everyday usage.

### Consideration vs Consideration

In law it is something of value exchanged between parties to legally bind a contract.

In everyday English it just means thinking carefully about something.

### Situationally Dependant

A lawyer reading a contract uses the precise legal meaning.

Everyone else uses the everyday meaning, often unaware of legal.

---

## Why Terms Of Art Matter

When a field uses a **term of art**, precision is the point.

### Ambiguity Is An Enemy

Using the word loosely introduces ambiguity.

Ambiguity causes miscommunication, misunderstanding, and bugs.

### Precision Is Your Friend

In programming, **terms of art** are the shared vocabulary developers use to reason about code.

When those terms drift, conversations break down and concepts become harder to learn.
