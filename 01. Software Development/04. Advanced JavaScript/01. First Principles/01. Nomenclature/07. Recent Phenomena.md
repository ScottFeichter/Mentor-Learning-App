# Recent Phenomena

&nbsp;

As of writing the current **buzz words** are anything to do with AI.

---

## Buzz Words In Programming

The tech industry produces **buzz words** constantly.

### Notable Buzz Words

- "cloud-native"
- "AI-powered"
- "blockchain"
- "disruption"
- "scalable"
- "full-stack"
- "agile"
- "10x engineer"

### Who Cares

Many of these started as precise **terms of art**.

Most have been stretched so far they no longer carry reliable meaning.

---

## Why This Matters

**Buzz words** are noise: when a word means everything it means nothing.

### Technical Maturity

Learning to recognize **buzz words** is a sign of technical maturity.

We want to be able to recognize and replace them with precise language.

### What To Do

When you hear a **buzz word**, ask:  *what does this actually mean in concrete terms?*

If the person using it (or no one) can answer that, it is a **buzz word**.
