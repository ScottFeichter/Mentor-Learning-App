# Words Matter

Especially when trying to learn and understand difficult topics.

## Term Of Art

The phrase **term of art** refers to a term that has a specialized meaning in a particular field or profession.

An example would be the term "hat trick" in the context of the sport hockey.

In hockey, this refers to a player scoring three goals in a single game.

However, this word originated in the mid 19th century in the context of a cricket.

In this context bowler taking three wickets successively and being presented a new hat by their club.

## Loose Language

People tend to use language in a convienient manner.

Also sometimes technical matters are not named with much aforethough.

And language is ever changing and evolving.

## What Is The Point?

The point is you will hear colleagues, even well meaning and knowledgeble ones, jumble terms of art.

It is easy to do and generally causes no harm because these topics are confusing, so don't hold it against them.

It could even be you doing it! :)
