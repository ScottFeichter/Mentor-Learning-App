# Taxonomy

&nbsp;

A **taxonomy** is a system for classifying things into named categories arranged in a hierarchy.

---

## Why Hierarchy

A hierarchy lets you reason about a whole category at once.

### Hierarchical Classification

With hierarchy, the classification knowledge flows down from parent to child.

Levels moves from broad to specific — each a subset of the one above it.

### The JS Syntax Taxonomy

The taxonomy in this unit classifies everything you write in JS.

This gives every piece of syntax a place and a reason for that place.

---

## Why It Gets Blurry

JS syntax does not always respect clean boundaries.

### Edge Cases

Edge cases in syntax classificaiton are not flaws in the taxonomy.

They reflect the nature of language syntax itself.

### Multiple Identity

A word in English can be a noun in one sentence and a verb in another.

JS syntax behaves similarly sometimes, though this behavior is limited.

---

## Category Levels

Categories are tools for thinking, not cages.

### Editorial Decisions

Some editorial decisions were made to allow this hierarchy to aid in learning.

Each level is clear and concise to facilitate easy transfer of understanding.

### The Goal

The goal of this **taxonomy** is not perfect discreteness — it is **useful clarity**.

When classification tension arises that tension is itself worth understanding.
