## Interfaces, Ports, and Connectors

- A **hardware port** is the external connection point for a bus interface
- A **bus** allows the transfer of data to and from devices
- A **connector** is the part of a peripheral cable inserted into a port
- Most connectors use edge contacts with **keying** (asymmetric design) or are reversible
- **COM Port** is Serial

## Binary Data Storage and Transfer Units

- **bit (b)** — binary digit, value of 1 or 0
- **byte (B)** — 8 bits
- Lowercase "b" = bit, uppercase "B" = byte

### Transfer Rates

| Multiplier | Bits | Bytes |
|-----------|------|-------|
| 1000 | Kb/s (Kilobits) | KB/s (Kilobytes) |
| 1000² | Mb/s (Megabits) | MB/s (Megabytes) |
| 1000³ | Gb/s (Gigabits) | GB/s (Gigabytes) |

## USB (Universal Serial Bus)

- Standard means of connecting most peripheral devices
- Managed by a host controller
- Up to 127 devices per controller (theoretical)
- Most motherboards have multiple USB controllers with 3–4 ports each
- Device classes: human interface, mass storage, printer, audio, etc.

## Storage Types

- **RAM** — non-persistent (volatile)
- **Mass Storage** — persistent (HDD, SSD)
- **Removable Storage** — persistent

## Key Interfaces

- **SATA** — Serial Advanced Technology Attachment (7-pin data, 15-pin power)
- **eSATA** — External SATA
- **M.2** — SSD adapter card form factor (supplies power over bus)
- **PCIe** — Peripheral Component Interconnect Express (point-to-point serial)
- **SCSI** — Small Computer System Interface
- **IDE/PATA** — Integrated Drive Electronics / Parallel ATA

## Motherboard Form Factors

- ATX, mATX, miniITX, nanoITX, picoITX, mobileITX

## Fan Connectors

- 3-pin fans can use 4-pin headers (may not vary speed)
- 4-pin fans work with 3-pin headers (no PWM)
